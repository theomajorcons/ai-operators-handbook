# Context Self-Check

Claude only works with what you put in front of it, and that space is limited.
Run this quick check before you send a prompt. The goal isn't to paste *more*;
it's to paste what's **relevant** and leave out the rest.

**Goal:** Confirm that everything Claude needs is present, and nothing it
doesn't need is wasting space, before you hit send.

---

## Did I include what Claude needs?

- [ ] **The actual material.** If the task is about an email, a draft, or some
  notes, did I paste them in? Without extra setup, Claude can't reach your files,
  inbox, or other chats; but if you've connected an integration (a connector,
  attachment, shared Project, tool, or enabled memory), Claude can access what
  that integration exposes. Know what your workspace has enabled before assuming
  nothing is reachable.
- [ ] **The facts that shape the answer.** Who it's for, what happened, what I
  want, and any hard constraints (length, tone, must-include).

## Did I leave out what Claude doesn't need?

- [ ] **Unrelated backstory.** Chit-chat, side stories, and how my week is going
  spend space and tell Claude nothing about the task. Cut them.
- [ ] **True-but-irrelevant detail.** Facts that are real but don't change *this*
  answer still dilute the signal. If it wouldn't change the output, drop it.
- [ ] **A second, different question.** If I've buried another ask in here, pull
  it out into its own prompt so neither gets a muddled answer.

---

**One-line rule of thumb:** *Longer isn't better. Put the relevant material in
the room with your question, and nothing else.*
