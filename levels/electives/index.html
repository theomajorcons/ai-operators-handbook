After this track you can…

  • ✓Run a research-and-publishing workflow where every material claim traces to a source you vetted, with a human review before it goes live.
  • ✓Show honestly whether an AI workflow saves time or money (counting review and cleanup) and compute its payback period.
  • ✓Introduce AI to a team with a small pilot, weekly feedback, data rules, and a pre-set date to expand, adjust, or stop.
  • ✓Put Claude to work on a spreadsheet by giving it column meanings and sample rows, checking every formula and number, and keeping sensitive data out.
  • ✓Let Claude act in a browser or desktop app safely by gating risky actions for a human, granting least access, and keeping a log you can check.

  1. Elective 1Public workflows: research and publishingConceptPublic work is safe only when you source every material factual claim to a page you opened and pass the draft through a human gate before it ships.8 min→
  2. Elective 2Measuring AI's impactConceptAn honest AI saving counts the review and cleanup time, not just the fast AI run, then turns the net weekly saving into a payback period kept in one unit.8 min→
  3. Elective 3Rolling AI out to a teamConceptA team rollout sticks when you start with a small volunteer pilot on one use case, set data rules and success, stop, and expand thresholds before day one, and decide on evidence by a fixed date.7 min→
  4. Elective 4Claude with spreadsheets and dataConceptClaude is useful on a spreadsheet only when you give it the column meanings and sample rows, check every formula and number it produces, try on a small slice first, and keep sensitive data out.9 min→
  5. Elective 5Claude in the browser and desktop appConceptWhen Claude acts in a browser or on your desktop it takes real actions on real pages and files, so you gate the risky ones for a human, give it the least access it needs, practice on safe targets first, and keep a log you can check.9 min→
