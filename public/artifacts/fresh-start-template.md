# Fresh start template

## Signs to restart
* Claude misses details you gave earlier, or relies on something you already corrected
* You hit your limit sooner than usual
* The chat has many files and many turns
* You are changing topic

## Step 1: ask Claude for the handoff
Paste this into the long chat:

"Write a handoff so I can continue in a new chat. Include: what we decided, what is still open, the key facts and numbers I will need, and which files matter. Keep it under 300 words."

## Step 2: check the handoff
Read it. Correct anything missing or wrong before you leave the old chat.

## Step 3: start the new chat
1. Paste the handoff first.
2. Attach only the files you still need.
3. Say what you want to do next.

Note: a fresh chat reduces the context carried forward. It does not reset your session or weekly usage limit.
