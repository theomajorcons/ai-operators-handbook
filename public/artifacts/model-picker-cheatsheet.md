## Three questions
1. One clear right answer? Fastest model, low effort.
2. Ordinary judgment? Middle model, default effort.
3. Hard, high stakes, or tangled? Try more effort first, then climb a tier.

## Then
* Read the result against the task.
* If it falls short, climb ONE step. Do not jump to the top.
* If money or people are affected, verify it yourself.
